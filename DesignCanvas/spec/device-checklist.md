# Design Canvas device checklist

Manual pass on real hardware. Nothing in this file is automated —
device-level end-to-end testing is explicitly deferred (see
`DesignCanvas/README.md` "Deferred"); this checklist is what stands in for
it until it exists. The Node half of the round lifecycle already has an
automated proof with no devices: `pnpm --dir DesignCanvas/server
test:e2e`, and the Mac-iPad link itself has one over a loopback socket
(`Tests/LinkLoopbackTests.swift`). This file is about everything those
cannot reach: usbmuxd and Bonjour, the actual screen-to-screen video path,
and the actual PencilKit/UIKit surface on a real iPad.

Run the whole list once over **USB** and once over **WiFi** — most checks
apply to both transports, but a bug in one binding rarely shows up in the
other.

## Setup

- A Mac running the Design Canvas Mac app (Screen Recording granted). The
  daemon and the Claude Code mod ship inside the app; only a Debug build
  made without pnpm needs **Settings ▸ Server build ▸ Choose…** pointed at
  `DesignCanvas/server/dist/index.js`.
- A real iPad running the Design Canvas iPad app, on iOS/iPadOS 16.4+.
- A real Claude Code session with the Design Canvas mod, started from the
  Mac app (**Open Project…**, then **Start**) or, with the every-session
  option on, by running `claude` in the project.
- Something with visible motion to freeze against (a video, a spinner, or
  a digital clock with seconds) for the freeze-exactness check.

## Checklist

| # | Check | How to verify |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Pairing** (changed 2026-11-03) | WiFi: the iPad appears under **iPads** in the Mac app's menu via Bonjour, with a **Connect** button; clicking it once starts the session, and relaunching the Mac app, or quitting and reopening the iPad app, reconnects that iPad with no further click (the click is what makes the Mac trust it over WiFi). **Disconnect** ends the session and stops the auto-reconnect. USB: plugging the cable connects with no discovery step and no click at all. An iPad only ever connected by cable is not dialed over WiFi on its own: unplug it, and after up to 15 s it shows under **iPads** as available over WiFi, waiting for a click. |
| 2 | **Mirror** | The iPad shows a live, low-latency mirror of the Mac's own screen — whatever is in front, letterboxed to the Mac's aspect ratio, with no extra display appearing in System Settings ▸ Displays. Tap and drag on the iPad's screen and confirm nothing moves, clicks, or scrolls on the Mac — a canvas session forwards no input in either direction. |
| 3 | **Zoom** | Pinch-to-zoom and pan on the iPad are smooth, local, and view-only: the Mac's display and the live mirror are unaffected by zooming in or out. |
| 4 | **Freeze exactness** | Put something moving on the Mac's screen (the clock/spinner). Enter Draw Mode at a moment you can identify precisely (e.g. "the second hand crosses 12"). After the round completes, open `~/.claude/channels/design-canvas/annotations/<id>/screenshot.png` for that annotation and confirm it shows that exact moment — not a frame noticeably before or after. |
| 5 | **Zoom in Draw Mode** (changed 2026-10-09; was "Zoomed crop") | Enter Draw Mode, then pinch and pan with two fingers: the frozen frame zooms and pans, and the fingers leave no dot or line behind. Draw at one zoom, zoom out and back in, and confirm every stroke stays on the part of the frame it was drawn on. Draw with the Pencil while your hand rests on the screen and confirm the picture does not zoom and the stroke is not cut short. Try to draw on a letterbox bar: nothing appears. Send, and confirm `composite.png` in that annotation's store folder is the full frame, not cropped to the zoomed region, with each stroke where it was drawn, and that the Claude Code event reads "Zoom region: full frame". Repeat once after zooming in *before* entering Draw Mode: the round is still the full frame. |
| 6 | **Send timing** | Tap Send and time how long until the round reaches Claude Code (the event appears in the Claude Code terminal). Target: under 1 s on a reasonably fast network (PRD success metric). |
| 7 | **Reply shown** (changed 2026-10-13; was the Agent Replies view) | Once Claude Code reports the outcome, a notification appears in the iPad's top-right corner without leaving or reopening the app: **Applied** (with the note, and "Open pull request" when a PR link was given, which opens it), **Couldn't apply** with Claude's message, or **Claude needs a reply at the desktop**. Applied leaves on its own after about 5 s; the other two stay until tapped or swiped away. With VoiceOver on, the notification is read out when it appears. |
| 8 | **Link loss mid-upload, with resend** | Draw a sketch, tap Send, and kill the link before the upload completes (unplug the USB cable, or turn off WiFi on the iPad). Confirm the sketch is not lost: Draw Mode enters RETRY, keeps the strokes, and resends automatically once the link comes back (the next successful `hello`). |
| 9 | **Rotation in Draw Mode** | Rotate the iPad while mid-sketch in Draw Mode. Confirm the strokes are kept, the session returns to LIVE (not silently discarded), and re-entering Draw Mode lets you finish and send. |
| 10 | **Reconnect snapshot** | With at least one round already replied, force a fresh reconnect (quit and relaunch the iPad app, or toggle airplane mode). Confirm the `rounds` snapshot sent after the new `hello` shows that round's correct final status immediately, with no live push needed. |
| 11 | **Connection state** (changed 2026-11-02; was "Both connection dots", then the 2026-10-13 pill) | With no Mac connected the iPad shows only the waiting screen; connecting replaces it with the mirror. The session pill names the target session's folder. Quit Claude Code (or Reset on the Mac) and confirm the pill reads "Choose a session" with an orange dot, the drawing button is greyed out, and the session menu says how to start a session. With two or more sessions, tap the pill: every session is listed with Ready, Busy or Waiting for reply at desktop, the target has a tick, and picking another moves the tick and the pill. End the target session while others remain and confirm the pill asks to "Choose a session" and Send waits for the pick. "Connection details…" opens the sheet with the connection parameters and the log. Then, with a Claude Code session the app did **not** start holding the channel, confirm the Mac menu says **Another session** in orange. |
| 12 | **Blank canvas** (added 2026-10-03, changed 2026-11-02; `PRD-BlankCanvas.md`) | With a session connected, tap the window button under the session pill: the menu lists the Mac by its own name (System Settings ▸ General ▸ Sharing) and **Whiteboard**. Pick Whiteboard: the mirror is replaced by a white page filling the screen, nothing changes in the Mac app's menu, and the tool panel is out at once, with no freeze delay and no drawing button. Sketch, add a note, tap Send. Confirm: the page clears and the tools stay out, ready for the next sketch; the reply notification follows; in the Claude Code terminal the event reads "New sketch from iPad (blank canvas)" with no "Zoom region" line; `composite.png` and `screenshot.png` in that annotation's store folder are the strokes on white and a plain white page, with no screen content, and the strokes are neither stretched nor offset. Draw again, then pick the Mac in the window menu: "End without sending?" asks first; end, and confirm the live picture is back with no ink on it and a normal mirror round still works. Quit and reopen the iPad app on the whiteboard: it is restored once the Mac reconnects, ready to draw. With no Mac connected, Whiteboard is greyed out and says it needs your Mac connected. |
| 13 | **Blank canvas, link loss** | On the whiteboard, draw, tap Send, and cut the link before the upload completes. Confirm the chip reads "Sketch kept — will resend", the iPad falls back to the mirror or the waiting screen while disconnected, and after reconnecting exactly one round arrives, still as a blank-canvas sketch. |
| 14 | **Panel docking** (added 2026-10-13) | In Draw Mode, drag the tool panel from anywhere on it to each of the four edges. While dragging, the grabber shows inside the panel, on the side facing into the screen from the edge the panel started on (along the top of a panel docked at the bottom), the panel does not grow for it, and it hides on release; the panel snaps to the edge nearest the drop. On a side edge its controls run top to bottom. Long-press the panel: no menu opens. Quit and relaunch the app: the panel is where it was left. With VoiceOver, the panel's position control offers Move to top, bottom, left and right, and each works. |
| 15 | **Top bar layout** (added 2026-10-13, changed 2026-11-03) | On an iPad in landscape, in Draw Mode on the mirror: the session pill at the top left with the window button under it, the prompt pill at the top right, level, and nothing overlaps. Dock the tool panel at the top: it takes the top row's centre; with a long folder name, or on an iPad mini, it takes the row below rather than running into the pill. In portrait it takes the row below. On an iPhone in portrait, the prompt pill takes the second row beside the window button, and a top-docked panel the third. Type a long note: the field stays one line and runs into nothing. With a reply notification showing, nothing overlaps in either orientation. |
| 16 | **Legibility** (added 2026-10-13) | In light and in dark appearance, over a dark mirror, a white one and the held print on its paper, read every piece of chrome: the session pill, the window button, the prompt pill and its placeholder, the tool panel, the drawing button, the kept-sketch chip, the notice, the notification, and the session and window menus. Nothing is faint or unreadable. Run once below iOS 26 if a device is available, where the glass falls back to the frosted material. |
| 17 | **Setup guide** (added 2026-10-27) | With no Mac connected, the waiting screen shows three steps: beside the title in landscape, under it in portrait, scrolling only where they do not fit. Tap **Copy**: it reads **Copied** for about 2 s, and VoiceOver says "Prompt copied". On a Mac signed in to the same Apple ID with Handoff on, paste into Claude Code: the text is exactly what the box shows. Run it with the Mac app installed and the every-session switch off. Claude Code adds the app's `Contents/Resources/mod` path to `env.CLAUDE_CODE_PLUGIN_DIRS` in `~/.claude/settings.json` and leaves the file's other keys and any other entries in that list alone. Relaunch the Mac app: Settings shows the switch on. A new `claude` session in any project connects. Turn the switch off: the entry is gone. Run the prompt on a Mac without the app: Claude Code stops and says it isn't installed, and the settings file is unchanged. |
| 18 | **Send animation** (added 2026-10-28, changed 2026-11-02) | With something moving on the Mac's screen, enter Draw Mode, draw, and tap Send. In under a second, the picture and its ink flash, lift off as a print in a white border and fly into the session pill, which swells a little as the card lands. The card shows the frozen picture and the ink exactly as they were, with none of the controls, while the live picture is already moving again behind it. Tap a control while the card is in flight: the tap goes through. Zoom in, draw and send: the card shows the zoomed view, and `composite.png` is still the full frame (check 5). On the whiteboard the card is white with a thin edge. With Reduce Motion on, the card only fades. With VoiceOver on, "Sending sketch" is read out. A sketch too large to send keeps Draw Mode open and shows no card. |
| 19 | **Session menu** (added 2026-11-02) | On an iPad and on an iPhone, in light and in dark appearance, tap the session pill with two or more sessions connected. The pill's glass grows from its top-left corner into the session menu. The session's name and status dot travel into that session's row, which has the tick, and land without a flicker or a doubled name. The chevron fades, and the other rows fade in as the glass settles. Nothing else in the top bar moves. Tap anywhere outside the menu: it runs backwards into the pill, and the tap reaches nothing else, neither the Mac nor the panel. Pick another session: the menu closes and that session's name and dot travel into the pill. Open it in Draw Mode: it opens from the pill, over the rest of the top bar, and stays on the screen. On an iPhone, with the panel in the row under the pill, the menu opens over the panel. With more sessions than fit (an iPhone in landscape), the rows scroll. With Reduce Motion on, the menu fades in and out and nothing travels. With VoiceOver on, focus moves into the menu when it opens, stays there, and the escape gesture closes it. |
| 20 | **Drawing button and tool panel** (added 2026-11-03) | On the mirror, a round pencil button with no words sits at the bottom left; with VoiceOver it reads "Start drawing". Tap it: the picture is held, the tool panel comes out, and the button becomes a cross ("End drawing"). On an iPad the panel is centred at the bottom; on an iPhone in portrait it shares the bottom row, right of the button; on an iPhone in landscape it is centred; nothing overlaps. Dock the panel on the left: it ends above the button. Tap the cross with no ink: drawing ends at once. Draw, then tap the cross: "End without sending?" grows from the button, above the panel. Tap outside it: you are still drawing, with the ink. Tap the cross again and **End without sending**: the ink is gone and the picture is live. Draw, tap Clear all in the panel: every stroke goes, and undo brings them back. On an iPhone, tap the ink swatch: the Ink Picker opens over it; pick an ink: it closes and the swatch shows that ink. |
| 21 | **Held picture as a print** (added 2026-11-03) | With something on the Mac's screen that reaches its edges (the menu bar, a window at the side), tap the pencil. The screen flashes, the picture shrinks a little, and the paper fills the screen around it: the Mac's menu bar and the windows at its edges are all in view, with none of them under the paper, and there is no "Mirroring paused" label. The paper is deeper at the bottom. Draw at the picture's edge: the stroke stops at the edge. Pinch out: zooming stops at that size. Pinch in toward a corner: the paper margin there grows with the picture, and further in, the picture fills the screen; pan toward an edge: it stops where the paper ends. Send, or tap the cross: the picture grows back to the fit as the paper goes, and the letterbox is black again. With Reduce Motion on, the picture changes size without moving. With VoiceOver on, "Picture frozen for drawing. Your Mac is still running." is read out. On the whiteboard nothing of this happens. |
| 22 | **Switching Macs** (added 2026-11-03) | With the iPad mirroring Mac A over the cable, click **Connect** for the iPad on Mac B over WiFi. The iPad asks "Switch Macs?", naming Mac B in quotes as a Mac calling itself that, and Mac A as the one on the cable; Mac B's row reads **Asking…**. Tap **Keep the Cable**: Mac A keeps mirroring and Mac B's row reads **In use**. Within the next minute, on Mac B, **Disconnect** and **Connect** again: the iPad does not ask, and Mac B's row reads **In use**. After a minute, do the same and tap **Switch**: Mac B mirrors and Mac A's row reads **In use**. Leave the question unanswered once: after 30 s it closes and the current Mac stays. Repeat with Mac A on WiFi instead of the cable: the button reads **Don't Switch**, and the message does not mention a cable. With one Mac only, turn the iPad's WiFi off and on mid-session: it reconnects on its own and the iPad asks nothing. Plug Mac B's cable in while Mac A mirrors over WiFi: Mac B takes over at once, without a question. |
| 23 | **Mismatched apps** (added 2026-11-03) | Pair a Mac app and an iPad app built from different wire versions (or an iPad app from before 2026-11-03). The Mac's row reads **Update needed**, and the iPad's waiting screen says which side to update; neither shows a mirror. |

## Reporting a failure

Log a fail with enough detail to file an issue: which check, which step of
the check, what was expected vs. observed, and — if it's plausibly a wire
bug rather than a UI bug — the relevant lines from the daemon's log
(`~/Library/Logs/DesignCanvas/server.log` by default, or wherever
`DESIGN_CANVAS_LOG_PATH` points) and the Mac app's own log,
`~/Library/Logs/DesignCanvas/designcanvas.log`. It fills with `DaemonClient:
rounds stream` lines whenever the daemon is down, so read it through
`grep -v "DaemonClient: rounds stream"`. The iPad's own log is under
Connection details ▸ Connection log, with a share button.
